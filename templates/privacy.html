Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and the expense entries you record, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the service: displaying your transactions, generating spending summaries, and maintaining your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting and infrastructure. These providers only process data as needed to support the service and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
